# Supply Agreement: Acme Components (Contract SA-2025-017)

## Scope
Acme Components supplies fasteners and mounting hardware (SKU-1001 steel hex bolts, SKU-1002 aluminium
mounting brackets) to WH-EAST and WH-WEST.

## Lead time and service level
- Standard lead time: 7 calendar days from purchase order acceptance.
- On-time delivery target: 95% of purchase orders per quarter.
- Expedited orders: 3 calendar days, at a 12% surcharge. Limited to 2,000 units per order.

## Minimum order quantities
- SKU-1001: 100 boxes per order.
- SKU-1002: 200 units per order.

## Late delivery penalties
If a delivery arrives more than 2 days after the confirmed delivery date, Acme credits 2% of the order
value for each additional day late, up to a maximum of 10%.

## Quality
Defect rate must stay below 0.5% per lot. Lots above that threshold may be returned at Acme's expense
within 30 days of receipt.
