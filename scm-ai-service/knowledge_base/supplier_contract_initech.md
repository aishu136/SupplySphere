# Supply Agreement: Initech Electronics (Contract SA-2025-031)

## Scope
Initech Electronics supplies electronic components (SKU-3001 temperature sensor modules, SKU-3002 IoT
gateway controller boards) to WH-EAST. Goods ship by ocean from Kaohsiung, Taiwan.

## Lead time and service level
- Standard lead time: 21 calendar days, including 14 days of ocean transit.
- Air freight option: 6 calendar days. The buyer pays freight at cost plus 5%.
- On-time delivery target: 90% of purchase orders per quarter.

## Minimum order quantities
- SKU-3001: 250 units per order.
- SKU-3002: 50 units per order.

## Late delivery penalties
Ocean shipments delayed by port congestion or weather are excluded from penalties. For other delays of
more than 5 days, Initech credits 1.5% of the order value per day, up to a maximum of 7.5%.

## Handling
Components ship in moisture-barrier bags with humidity indicator cards. If a humidity indicator shows
more than 10% RH on arrival, the buyer may reject the lot.
