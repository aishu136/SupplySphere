# Damaged Goods Receiving Procedure (SOP-RCV-011)

## Inspection
Every inbound pallet is photographed at the receiving dock. The vision inspection checks for crushed
corners, tears, punctures, water damage and broken stretch wrap, and reads the shipment label.

## When damage is detected
1. Mark the shipment DAMAGED in the system. Do not receive the goods into available stock.
2. Move the pallet to the quarantine area (bay Q1 in WH-EAST, bay Q3 in WH-WEST).
3. Photograph every damaged carton and attach the images to the shipment record.
4. Notify the carrier within 24 hours of delivery. Claims filed after 24 hours are usually rejected.
5. Notify the supplier's account contact within 48 hours if damage appears to come from packing rather than transit.

## Severity
- Minor: outer carton scuffed or dented, contents intact. Goods can be received after a manual check.
- Major: carton torn or crushed, or contents exposed. Every unit must be inspected; damaged units go to returns.
- Critical: water damage, or a collapsed pallet. Reject the whole delivery.

## Electronics
For Electronics category SKUs, treat any major damage as critical. Moisture-sensitive components cannot be
received if packaging shows any sign of water exposure.
